---
term: 物の哀れ
reading: もののあわれ
romaji: mono no aware
meaning: the gentle ache of things passing — beauty made sharper because it ends
register: formal
chineseNote: >-
  Chinese renders it 物哀 (wù'āi), borrowed back from Japanese to name the
  Japanese idea. 哀 alone in Chinese is plain grief; the Japanese sense is
  softer and more appreciative than sad.
---
