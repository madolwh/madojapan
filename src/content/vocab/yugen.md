---
term: 幽玄
reading: ゆうげん
romaji: yūgen
meaning: a deep beauty that is felt rather than stated — the power of what is left unsaid
register: formal
chineseNote: >-
  幽玄 (yōuxuán) is classical Chinese for "deep and obscure", a fairly plain
  description. Japanese kept the characters and turned them into an aesthetic
  standard for nō theatre and poetry.
---
