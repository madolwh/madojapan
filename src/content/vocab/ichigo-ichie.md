---
term: 一期一会
reading: いちごいちえ
romaji: ichigo ichie
meaning: one time, one meeting — treat every encounter as unrepeatable
register: formal
chineseNote: >-
  The four characters are ordinary Chinese ones, but the phrase is Japanese.
  A Chinese speaker can read it and still not recognise it as an idiom — the
  meaning lives in the tea ceremony it came from, not in the characters.
---
