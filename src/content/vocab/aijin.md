---
term: 愛人
reading: あいじん
romaji: aijin
meaning: a lover, an affair
description: Specifically someone you should not be with.
register: polite
chineseMeaning: husband or wife
chineseNote: >-
  In Chinese 愛人 (àiren, simplified 爱人) is your husband or wife. Introducing
  your spouse as your 愛人 in Japan tells the room you are having an affair.
  Same two characters, opposite respectability.
---
