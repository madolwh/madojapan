---
term: 手紙
reading: てがみ
romaji: tegami
meaning: a letter
description: The kind you put in an envelope and post.
register: polite
chineseMeaning: toilet paper
chineseNote: >-
  In Chinese 手紙 (shǒuzhǐ, simplified 手纸) is toilet paper. Writing that you
  are sending someone 手紙 reads very differently across the two languages.
